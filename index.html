The plan for the day

The goal is the summit, and the day is paced around the prayers: Dhuhr on a break on the way up, Asr at the top, and Maghrib back at the lot. Everyone is down before sunset.

  1. 10–11 AM

    Meet at the Bear Mountain Inn lot. Bathrooms, water and wudu here.

  2. ~11 AM

    Start the hike on the trail that wins the vote.

  3. 12:40 PM

    Dhuhr. Break partway up to pray and eat lunch.

  4. ~3:15 PM

    Reach the summit at Perkins Tower. Rest and take in the view.

  5. 3:34 PM

    Asr at the summit (Shafi’i and others; 4:19 PM Hanafi), then start down.

  6. ~5:30 PM

    Everyone back down at the lot, with a buffer before dark.

  7. 6:01 PM

    Sunset. Maghrib together, about 10–15 minutes.

  8. ~6:20 PM

    Head home, or join dinner if you're up for it.

Prayer times are for Bear Mountain on Oct 25. Asr is 3:34 PM for Shafi’i and others, and 4:19 PM by the Hanafi calculation. Either way we pray at the summit; Hanafi folks start down right after and are still back before sunset. Bring water for wudu on the trail. On the Four Peaks route we pass Bear’s summit early, so Asr would be on the trail instead.

      Good to know

      Arrive early if driving

      Bear Mountain fills up on fall weekends. On Oct 18 the park hit capacity and closed to new cars, and roadside parking isn't allowed. Aim for the lot by 10 AM. Parking is $10 per car.

      Bring

      Shoes with real grip (the stone steps and rock slabs get slick), 1–2 liters of water plus a little extra for wudu, lunch for the Dhuhr break, a warm layer for the summit, a headlamp just in case, and something to pray on.

      Trail detours

      Construction near the Inn has rerouted the start of the Appalachian, Major Welch and Suffern–Bear Mountain trails. Follow the posted detour signs; the group will start together.
